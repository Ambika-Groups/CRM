1. Scope of work

Construction of a ground-plus-two (G+2) residential house on plot no. 27, Sector 6, Gomti Nagar Extension, Lucknow, built-up area 2,450 sq ft, as per the approved map and the bill of quantities attached as Annexure A.

2. Contract value

Lump sum of ₹48,50,000 (Rupees Forty-Eight Lakh Fifty Thousand only), plus GST as applicable. The Contractor supplies materials, labour and equipment unless Annexure B says otherwise.

StageShareAmount (₹)

4. Time and delay

Work starts on 1 Nov 2026 and completes by 31 Oct 2027. If the Contractor is late for reasons within its control, it pays ₹2,000 per day of delay, up to 5% of the contract value (₹2,42,500).

Changed for this contract: the template says ₹1,500 per day.

5. Other terms

  1. Retention: 5% of each stage payment is held until the end of the defect liability period of 12 months after handover.
  2. Variations and extra work are priced at the rates in Annexure C, or agreed in writing before the work is done.
  3. Safety, insurance and labour-law compliance on site are the Contractor's responsibility.
  4. Force majeure, termination, and dispute resolution by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Courts at Lucknow have jurisdiction.
